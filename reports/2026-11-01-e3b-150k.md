## Method and limits

Episodes are assigned by cumulative observed-step endpoint; only whole completed episodes are included. Counts and actual selected steps are shown. These are online training trajectories of changing policies, one seed and uncontrolled layouts; not frozen-policy evaluation or evidence of causation. Cumulative observed steps are distinct from saved model steps and may include unsaved tails across sessions. Baseline `col_e3b` is absent, shown as unavailable rather than zero; reward columns are not performance scores. `rooms_cleared` is the number recorded in the episode CSV, not a separate frozen-evaluation metric. Full-batch structured has only 22,535 observed steps and is compared only at `(0,22535]`; GPU auxiliary stopped at model step 141,312 and is compared at no more than `(0,141312]`. No extrapolation.

Episode CSV can include unfinished-rollout tails, so episode endpoints do not prove exact policy/model step parity.
