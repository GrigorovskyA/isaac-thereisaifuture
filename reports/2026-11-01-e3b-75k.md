## Method and limits

Episodes are assigned by cumulative observed-step endpoint; only whole completed episodes are included. Counts and actual selected steps are shown. These are online training trajectories of changing policies, one seed and uncontrolled layouts; not frozen-policy evaluation or evidence of causation. Cumulative observed steps are distinct from saved model steps and may include unsaved tails across sessions. Baseline `col_e3b` is absent, shown as unavailable rather than zero; reward columns are not performance scores. `rooms_cleared` is the number recorded in the episode CSV, not a separate frozen-evaluation metric.
